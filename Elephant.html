Incredible Animals

Able to lift an incredible 6,000 kg, the African Bush Elephant is the animal capable of lifting the most weight.

Stats

  • Name: African Bush Elephant
  • Max Speed: 40 km/hr
  • Size: 10 m
  • Weight: 4,500 to 7,000 kg
  • Fun facts Males continue growing until they are 35 to 40 years old.
  • Lifespan: 80 Years
