Blue Whale

Weighing in at a whopping 300 tonnes and with a maximum recorded size of 110 ft, the Blue Whale is the largest and heaviest animal on Earth.

Stats

  • Name: Blue Whale
  • Max Speed: 50 km/h
  • Size: 40 m
  • Weight: 300 t
  • Lifespan: 80–90 years
